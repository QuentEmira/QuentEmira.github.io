It is a warning to be careful of props or things in a story or play that might raise the expectation and anticipation of the viewer due to its presence, even though its got nothing important to do.

Eg: When a person coughs in a movie, it is usually used to signify that they are going to get sick, or die, or there is something wrong with their surroundings. No one in a movie coughs in random. But in real life, people cough in random all the time.

It also acts as a warning, as stories are constructed in such a way that ambiguity is intentionally left away, whereas in real life, nothing exists without ambiguity.
