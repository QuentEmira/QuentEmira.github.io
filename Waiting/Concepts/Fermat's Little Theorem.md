Fermat's Little Theorem states the below:
If $a$ and $p$ are numbers, such that $p$ is a prime that does not divide $a$, then:
$$a^{p-1} \equiv 1\ \textrm{mod}\ p $$
or
$$a^{p} \equiv a\ \textrm{mod}\ p $$

This can be used to simplify the process of fast exponentiation. This can also be used to find the multiplicative inverse of a number.
