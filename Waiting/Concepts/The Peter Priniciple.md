The principle is simple:

	In a company, eventually every employee will be promoted to a position of
	incompetence.

The idea here being that people take promotion as it denotes growth, but often are not aware of their limitations.

This idea is meant to be satirical.

#### Peter Parry
It is the situation that when a person identifies that he has reached his limits, and he gets an offer for a promotion, he must be careful about declining it, as it can have unintended negative consequences due to the presence of social tension.

#### Creative Incompetence
This is a way to counter the peter principle. The idea is to show that you are creatively incompetent in things that don't hinder your main work, but still makes your boss not choose you. This could be in the form of wearing a peppa pig tie for a meeting, and other things.

The idea here is to not get offered the promotion when you realise that getting promoted leads you to have skills you are not capable of getting.

#### Is this idea helpful?
I can see the logic behind this satire, but is it helpful? Once you find your limits, should you accept them, should you try pushing them? What would I choose?

By respecting them, I can see that we would be reliable socially, be happy with what we do, and can do the job well. But there will also be that thought of can't I do more? That thought in itself, will it go away?

By pushing past one's limits, or atleast attempting to, one can improve and reach new heights. But one can also fail miserably. I could lead to potential harm too. They are the limits of a person for a reason.
