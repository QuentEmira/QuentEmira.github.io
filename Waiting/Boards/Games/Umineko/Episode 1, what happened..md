### Magic is Risk
- This comes from the opinions of Kinzo. In the game, Kinzo says that magic, and thus miracles, only comes to those who are willing to risk a lot.
- Risk directly increases the effectiveness of the magic or miracle, and thus the effect and its extent.
- Reviving the witch is a miracle, which needs great risk. An important part of reviving her is the challenge of finding the hidden gold. So by putting it in an epitaph under her painting for all to see, and thus be eligible to find and own the gold for themselves, he has increase the risk, and thus increase the chances of the miracle succeeding.
- I am not clear about this completely, but this seems to be the gist.
