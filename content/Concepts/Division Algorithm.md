The division algorithm is a representation of the division operation of two number, where the dividend is represented as a form of the divisor

Division Algorithm
```
Given that a and b are two numbers, and that b != 0.

a can be represented as:

a = (b * q) + r
where,
a = Dividend
b = Divisor
q = Quotient
r = Remainder
```

- This is a very standard way of representing things
- This can be written in another way: $a = (b * floor(a/b) ) + a mod b$.
